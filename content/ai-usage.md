How AI tools are (and aren't) used at Curated Forest — both in building the
stack and in the documentation you're reading now.

## In the documentation

Parts of this site are drafted with the help of AI assistants. Anything
published has been reviewed for accuracy, but treat draft pages as
work-in-progress notes rather than authoritative reference.

## In the stack

AI and ML tooling that runs as part of Curated Forest, where applicable —
local inference, assistants, and any automation that leans on models. The
guiding principle mirrors the rest of the stack: prefer self-hosted,
open-weight options that keep data local over cloud APIs.

## Principles

- **Local-first.** Prefer models that run on-site over sending data to a
  third party.
- **Human-in-the-loop.** AI drafts and suggests; a person reviews and
  decides.
- **Transparency.** Where AI meaningfully shaped a page or a system, say so.
