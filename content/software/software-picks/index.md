The open-source software that makes up the Curated Forest stack, and why
each one earned its place. Most of these have their own dedicated sections
under [Technology](/tech/) — this page is the map.

## Core platform

- **[Kubernetes](/tech/kubernetes/) (k3s)** — lightweight orchestration for
  the whole cluster.
- **Linux** — the base OS on every node.

## Automation & devices

- **[Home Assistant](/tech/home-assistant/)** — the automation brain.
- **[ESPHome](/software/farm/esphome/)** — firmware for the ESP32 sensor/actuator
  nodes.

## Data & observability

- **PostgreSQL + [TimescaleDB](/software/farm/timescaledb/)** — time-series storage.
- **[Grafana](/software/farm/grafana/)** — dashboards and visualization.

## Storage & backups

- **[SeaweedFS](/software/shared/seaweed-fs/)** — distributed object storage.
- **[Kopia](/software/shared/kopia-backups/)** — encrypted, deduplicated off-site
  backups.
- **[Immich](/software/productivity/immich/)** — self-hosted photo and video library.
