## Fill volume
1. Empty bucket
2. Fill bucket with 500 ml. 
3. Let settle
4. Take calibration reading.
5. Repeat a few times.
6. Run to empty 
7. Confirm reasonably close to 0, or record as 0

## EC calibration
1. Does water 1
2. Add 1 dose Part A 
3. Manually mix
4. Run pump to bleed old water.
5. Take EC
   
6. Dose water 1
7. Add 1 dose Part B 
8. Manually mix
9. Run pump to bleed old water.
10. Take EC
