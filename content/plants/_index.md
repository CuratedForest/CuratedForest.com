At this point, I've killed more plants than I've kept alive successfully, though that's trending the right direction finally. Plants are fun because they're easy to grow in poor conditions, and challenging to get all their parameters right and really have growth take off. 

I believe [aeroponics](aeroponics/_index.md) provides a generic way to improve plant growth and am excited to be experimenting with it. I focus mostly on [strawberries](strawberries/_index.md), but do grow a handful of other plants: passion fruit, dragon fruit, house plants, and sometimes potatoes. 

My hope for this section is to document all the best things I've learned and found around aeroponics, strawberries, and plants generally. For other resources I've found, stop by these pages:
- [Aeroponic Resources](aeroponics/resources/_index.md)
- [Strawberry Resources](strawberries/resources/_index.md)
- [Plant Resources](resources/_index.md)

If you have a resource or other contribution, please feel free to use the edit button at the top of this page and create a pull request.
